[size=5][b][font=Comic Sans MS]Character Creator Enhanced[/font]
[/b][/size]
[b]Every option of Character Creator in the game's own barber screen, with the game's own tabs, lists, sliders and preview, for Kliff, Damiane and Oongka.[/b]

Your choices are normal barber changes, kept in your save. There is no extra menu to learn: visit a barber and choose.

[size=4][b]Two editions - install one[/b][/size]
• [b]Barber (recommended)[/b]: everything at the game's own barber, as described on this page
• [b]Anywhere[/b]: an editor panel of its own that opens anywhere with [b]F6[/b] (Kliff), [b]F7[/b] (Damiane) or [b]F8[/b] (Oongka), with no barber needed. Your look is kept per character outside your saves. Its full instructions are in its README

[size=4][b]At the barber[/b][/size]
• [b]Gender and race[/b]: male or female, Human, Orc, Dwarf or Goblin, for each of the three characters
• [b]Head[/b]: every head of the character's gender, from all four races
• [b]Face Details[/b]: mix features of a second head into the chosen one, for many more faces
• [b]Face Shape[/b]: 24 sliders on any head - face and jaw width, cheekbones, chin, nose, mouth and lips, eyes, brows, ears. Only your character changes, not NPCs
• [b]Body[/b]: bodies of the character's race and gender, skin colour, shine, brightness and subsurface
• [b]Body Shape[/b]: 19 sliders, with their own bones for men and women - chest or bust, shoulders, arms, biceps, triceps, forearms, neck, waist, belly, hips, glutes, thighs, quads, calves. Clothes and armor follow. Only your character changes, not NPCs
• [b]Height[/b]: from -20% to +20%, shown live
• [b]Hair[/b]: every hair style of every race and gender, colour, length, shine, hair tips and inner hair colours
• [b]Beard[/b]: every beard for men, colour, length
• [b]Eyebrows[/b]: colour, length, brow makeup
• [b]Eyes[/b]: 21 eye colours, from natural browns, blues and greens to red, gold, ice and cat eyes, plus iris brightness, tint strength, size and pupil size; [b]Eyelashes[/b]: colour, opacity
• [b]Makeup[/b]: lips, eyeliner, eyeshadow with glitter, blush, highlighter and contour
• [b]Face and body tattoos[/b]: type, colour, opacity, position, rotation and size
• [b]Scars[/b]: face and body, with colour, opacity, position and size
• [b]Paint and dirt[/b]: face and body war paint and grime, with colour, opacity, position and size
• [b]Icons[/b]: every hair, beard, head and body has a picture drawn from its own model on one mannequin, in the style of the game's barber icons. New tabs have icons in the game's style too
• [b]Female Armor Fit[/b] (optional download): male armor reshaped for a woman's body - bust, waist, shoulders, arms - used only when a woman wears it; men keep the original look

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
• [b]DMM 4.5 or newer[/b] (recommended) - it installs everything, including the .asi and the ASI loader. Older versions can't mount mods for the game's DLC update
• Or: another mod manager for the game files, plus the [b]Ultimate ASI Loader[/b] in the game's bin64 folder

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[b]First remove the previous version:[/b] uninstall the old "Character Creator" package (or the other edition) in your mod manager, delete [i]Character Creator.asi[/i] from [i]<game>\bin64[/i] if it is still there, and remove [i]Female Animations.field.json[/i] if you use it (the mod switches gender itself).

[b]With DMM (recommended)[/b]
1. Import the downloaded zip in DMM as it is (don't unzip and re-zip it).
2. Make sure all parts are enabled: [b]Character Creator Enhanced - Barber / Character Creator Enhanced[/b] and the [b]Equip All Armor[/b] module in the mod list, and [b]CharacterCreator[/b] in the ASI plugins.
3. Click [b]Apply[/b]. DMM installs the game files, places [i]CharacterCreator.asi[/i] in bin64 and sets up the ASI loader.
4. Start the game, load a save and visit a barber.

[b]Manual install[/b] (another mod manager, or no .asi support)
1. Install the game files with your mod manager as usual: the [b]Character Creator Enhanced[/b] folder inside [b]Character Creator Enhanced - Barber[/b] holds the 0009, 0010 and 0012 folders ([i]Equip All Armor.json[/i] is a DMM mod: every character can wear each other's armor).
2. Install the [b]Ultimate ASI Loader[/b] into [i]<game>\bin64[/i] if you do not have it yet (many mods use it, usually as winmm.dll).
3. Copy [i]CharacterCreator.asi[/i] from the [b]Character Creator Enhanced - Barber[/b] folder into [i]<game>\bin64[/i].
4. Start the game, load a save and visit a barber.

On its first start the plugin creates [i]<game>\bin64\CharacterCreator[/i] with its data; the log is kept there too.

[size=4][b]Changing gender or race[/b][/size]
The skeleton is chosen when the game starts, so these need a restart:
1. Choose at the barber and press [b]Save[/b] there.
2. Save the game, restart it and load that save.
3. Press [b]Save[/b] at the barber once more. Until then the barber and shop previews flash the old look for a moment.
Body, head and beard are changed to ones that fit. A woman has the female animations. A save with no choice stored keeps the last one chosen; a save that has one applies it at the next restart.

[size=4][b]Female Armor Fit (optional)[/b][/size]
A separate download in the Files tab. Most armor in the game only exists for men, and on a woman it looked broad, flat and had wobbly arms. With Female Armor Fit, over 1000 male armor parts (chest, legs, gloves, boots, helmets, shoulders, cloaks...) are reshaped to the female body: bust, narrower waist and shoulders, arms and sleeves that fit. Armor that has a version Damiane can wear uses the game's own women's version. The game switches only while a woman (any race) wears the piece - men and NPCs keep the original.
1. Import [i]Female Armor Fit[/i] in DMM next to Character Creator and click [b]Apply[/b]. Keep its [i]Dyes[/i] part enabled, so reshaped armor can be dyed.
2. Don't use it together with mods that also replace the game's part table (for example Cloak Remover) or that swap the same armor pieces to other armor for women (for example my Female Armor Module) - use one or the other.
3. It is made for the current game version. After a game update, disable it until an updated version is out.
4. Update it together with Character Creator: women's weapons and shields need the matching versions (Character Creator 9.1.18 or newer with Female Armor Fit 1.0.6).
A few pieces with unusual models still keep their male shape.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
• [b]Height[/b] shows at once, in the preview too. Until you restart, arms and shoulders look off while moving; after the restart the character is built at that height and moves normally.
• [b]A new head can show dark patches[/b] on the skin until you press Save and restart: the skin is laid out for the head the character was built with.
• [b]Eye colour[/b]: the head is swapped for about a second while the eyes reload. Each character has their own copy of the heads and eyes, so NPCs keep their own eye colour.
• [b]Face and Body Shape[/b]: the shape shows a moment after you stop moving a slider. 50 is the head's or body's own shape; the slider goes from 0 to 100, not below zero. A few bodies in the list have no shape to change.
• [b]"Others" is greyed[/b] while a list is on its first option, as the game does for its own tabs.
• [b]Heads of other races[/b] were made for their own race's body, so some may not sit perfectly at the neck.
• [b]Lip sync[/b]: a character played as the other gender keeps their own mouth movements in dialogue, matching what they say.
• [b]Gliding[/b]: each character glides their own way whatever their gender - Kliff with the crow wings, Damiane with her glider, Oongka with his rocket. If another mod handles gliding, set [i]Glide = 0[/i] under [i][Fixes][/i] in [i]<game>\bin64\CharacterCreator.ini[/i] (DMM's ASI config can edit it) and restart the game.
• [b]Two-handed swords as a woman[/b]: rolls move the whole body, and the sword sits in the right hand for Kliff's attacks and in the left for her own moves. Both fixes come from sevnupp's [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/crimsondesert/mods/3477]Female Longsword Fix[/url] - thank you! [i]TwoHandGrip = 0[/i] under [i][Fixes][/i] switches the grip fix off.
• [b]New tabs are in English[/b]; the game's own words stay in your language.
• [b]After a game update[/b] the new tabs switch off, and the barber keeps its own, until an updated version is out.
• [b]Other mods[/b] that replace the game's decorationparam files, texture palette or cd_item_icon.xml don't work together with this edition.
• [b]Coming from the Anywhere edition:[/b] its look, gender, race and height are not used by this edition. Its files stay in [i]<game>\bin64\CharacterCreator[/i] for when you go back.
• [b]Uninstalling:[/b] saves remember body, head, hair and beard by their number in the mod's lists, so without the mod a character may pick up a different one. A barber visit sets it again.
• [b]CharacterCreator.asi disappeared or flagged as a virus?[/b] Your antivirus removed it by mistake - a plugin that hooks into the game looks suspicious to them. Restore it (Windows: Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Protection history) and add exclusions for [b]both[/b] the game folder ([i]...\steamapps\common\Crimson Desert[/i]) and your [b]DMM folder[/b] - DMM keeps its own copy of the .asi in its mods folder and copies it into the game on every Apply. (Windows: Windows Security > Virus & threat protection > Manage settings > Exclusions > Add an exclusion > Folder.)

[size=4][b]Bug reports[/b][/size]
Please attach [i]<game>\bin64\CharacterCreator\CharacterCreator.log[/i]. If you suspect the new tabs cause a crash, create a text file [i]disable.txt[/i] in that folder containing the word [i]barbertabs[/i] - it gives you the game's own barber tabs - and tell me whether the crash is gone.
